# Tutorials and Learning Resources

## 1. Hugging Face Course

A structured introduction to transformer models, NLP, datasets, and the modern LLM ecosystem.

https://huggingface.co/learn/nlp-course/

## 2. Stanford CS25: Transformers United

Lectures and talks covering transformer architectures and modern language-model research.

https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs25/

## 3. Stanford CS224N

Graduate-level material on natural language processing with deep learning, including transformer-based NLP.

https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/

## 4. Retrieval-Augmented Generation Survey

Useful research reading for understanding RAG concepts, architectures, and limitations.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10997

## 5. Crossref Documentation

Practical documentation for understanding DOI metadata and scholarly reference infrastructure.

https://www.crossref.org/documentation/
