# Datasets and Benchmarks

> **Important:** These are candidate resources relevant to the topic. Verify the current official source, availability, license, and scope before final submission.

## 1. SciReviewGen

**Source:** Research project / arXiv publication  
**Description:** A large-scale dataset for automatic literature-review generation containing human-written literature reviews and cited-paper information.  
**Application:** Training and evaluating literature-review generation and retrieval-grounded review systems.  
**Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15186

## 2. LLMLagBench

**Source:** Research project / arXiv publication  
**Description:** A benchmark designed to investigate temporal training boundaries and knowledge lag in large language models.  
**Application:** Evaluating how model knowledge aligns with publication/event time.   
**Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.12116

## 3. Dated-Data / Temporal Knowledge Evaluation Resources

**Source:** Research associated with *Dated Data: Tracing Knowledge Cutoffs in Large Language Models*  
**Description:** Resources and evaluation methodology for probing the temporal boundaries of LLM knowledge.  
**Application:** Studying effective knowledge cutoffs and temporal knowledge behavior.  
**Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12958
